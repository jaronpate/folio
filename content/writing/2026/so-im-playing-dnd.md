It would behove me to start this with a disclaimer or two: for one this is ADnD 1st edition, :margin-note[Daniel will prove you wrong if you think ADnD 1e is bad] and two I am 2 weeks and 2 live sessions deep. Actual fresh meat.

For a long time Dungeons & Dragons has been a mystical thing to me. I didn't know how it works or what gameplay looked like. It was just a meme that people with thicker glasses than me play it.

That said, all I have to do to play is state what I want my character to do and sometime roll dice for randome numbers. :margin-note[Haha a deceptively simple explanation of the game!]

Then I lie at the mercy of our omniscient and benevolent DM who adjudicates my actions. Daniel has been the best DM I have ever had so far. Get it? No really though, he spends a lot of time and energy world building for us and even has custom software. :margin-note[(its multi-platform too...ooooo ahhhh)]
